# Standard types

These types come with every program out of the box: numbers and text,
arrays and lists, references, and nullability. Read these before moving
on to [Custom types](./custom-types).

- [Builtin types](./builtin-types) are numbers, booleans, and characters.
- [Arrays](./arrays) are fixed-size sequences, including slices.
- [List](./list) is a resizable array.
- [Strings](./strings) are text.
- [Vector math](./vector-math) does element-wise arithmetic on fixed-size arrays.
- [References and pointers](./references) borrow values and address memory.
- [Nullable types](./nullable-types) may or may not hold a value.
